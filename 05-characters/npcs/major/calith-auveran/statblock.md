## Statblock

### Calith Auveran

*Medium Humanoid (Elf, Human, Bard), Chaotic Good*

**AC** 20 (Bracers of Defense, *Mage Armor*) &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; **Initiative** +12 (22)
**HP** 289 (34d8 + 136)
**Speed** 30 ft.

| | MOD | SAVE | | MOD | SAVE | | MOD | SAVE |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|:---:|:---:|---|:---:|:---:|
| **Str** 10 | +0 | +0 | **Dex** 20 | +5 | +12 | **Con** 18 | +4 | +4 |
| **Int** 20 | +5 | +5 | **Wis** 22 | +6 | +13 | **Cha** 30 | +10 | +17 |

**Skills** Arcana +12, History +19, Insight +20, Perception +20, Performance +24, Persuasion +24
**Resistances** Cold, Psychic
**Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
**Gear** Bracers of Defense, Instrument of the Bards (Ollamh Harp), Ring of Spell Storing
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Truesight 120 ft.; Passive Perception 30
**Languages** All; telepathy 120 ft.
**CR** 24 (XP 62,000; PB +7)

#### Traits

***Legendary Resistance (4/Day).*** If Calith fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

***Magic Resistance.*** Calith has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Nameless.*** Calith gave up his name in 1389. Divination magic can't target him by name, and he can't be perceived by the Unwoven. This trait ends for 24 hours if his true name is written in a Concord Ledger.

***Song Without Address.*** Calith's magic doesn't rely on the Weave or on any god, so it ignores raw-magic surges, antimagic that targets the Weave, and the silence of the gods.

#### Actions

***Multiattack.*** Calith makes three Sundered Chord attacks. He can replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting.

***Sundered Chord.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +17, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 32 (4d10 + 10) Psychic damage.

***The Unfinished Measure (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 25, each enemy in a 60-foot Emanation originating from Calith. *Failure:* 88 (16d10) Psychic damage, and the target has the Incapacitated condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.

***Spellcasting.*** Calith casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 25):

**At Will:** *Detect Magic*, *Mage Armor* (included in AC), *Message*, *Minor Illusion*, *Prestidigitation*

**2/Day Each:** *Counterspell* (level 9 version), *Greater Invisibility*, *Teleport*

**1/Day Each:** *Foresight*, *Power Word Heal*, *Power Word Kill*, *True Polymorph*

#### Bonus Actions

***Bardic Inspiration (5/Day).*** One ally within 60 feet of Calith adds 1d12 to its next d20 Test within 10 minutes.

#### Reactions

***Cutting Words.*** *Trigger:* A creature Calith can see within 60 feet makes an attack roll, an ability check, or a damage roll. *Response:* Calith subtracts 1d12 from the roll, possibly causing it to fail.

#### Legendary Actions

*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, Calith can expend a use to take one of the following actions. He regains all expended uses at the start of his turn.*

***Refrain.*** Calith makes one Sundered Chord attack.

***Grace Note.*** Calith teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space he can see.

***Late Word.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 25, one creature Calith can see within 120 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Stunned condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* The target can't be targeted by Late Word again for 24 hours.

**Habitat:** Arctic (Black Still, Charaxis) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Treasure:** Relics
