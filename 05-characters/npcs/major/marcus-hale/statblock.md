## Paladin Class Features (2024)

### Fighting Style (Level 2)

**Defense**

+1 AC while wearing armor (already included above).

---

### Divine Smite (2024 Version)

- **Once per turn**, when Marcus hits with a **weapon attack**
- Expends a **spell slot**
- Deals **Radiant damage**
- +1d8 per slot level (max as defined by PHB)
- +1d8 vs Fiends or Undead

> ⚠️ No longer unlimited per turn. This is enforced.
> 

---

### Lay on Hands (Level 1)

- **40 HP pool**
- Can heal or cure disease/poison

---

### Channel Divinity (2 uses)

### ⚔️ Vow of Enmity (Bonus Action)

- Choose one creature within 10 ft
- **Advantage on attack rolls** against it for **1 minute**
- No concentration

### ⚔️ Abjure Enemy (Action)

- One creature within 60 ft
- WIS save or **Frightened**
- Speed becomes **0** if frightened

---

### Extra Attack (Level 5)

Marcus attacks **twice** when he takes the Attack action.

---

### Aura of Protection (Level 6)

- Marcus and allies within **10 ft**
- Add **+3 (CHA)** to **all saving throws**

---

### Sacred Oath Feature – Relentless Avenger (Vengeance)

When Marcus hits a creature with an **Opportunity Attack**, he can move **half his speed** without provoking opportunity attacks.

- When Marcus **rolls Hit Dice to regain hit points**, he **regains double the number of hit points** rolled.
