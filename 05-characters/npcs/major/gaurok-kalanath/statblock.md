### Gaurok "Kapı" Kalanath

*Medium Humanoid (Goliath, Fighter), Neutral Good*

**AC** 19 &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; **Initiative** +1 (11)
**HP** 75 (10d8 + 30)
**Speed** 35 ft.

| | MOD | SAVE | | MOD | SAVE | | MOD | SAVE |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|:---:|:---:|---|:---:|:---:|
| **Str** 20 | +5 | +8 | **Dex** 12 | +1 | +1 | **Con** 16 | +3 | +6 |
| **Int** 13 | +1 | +1 | **Wis** 10 | +0 | +0 | **Cha** 8 | −1 | −1 |

**Skills** Athletics +8, Perception +3, Survival +3
**Gear** Bag of Holding, Halberd +1, Javelins (4), Manacles (4), Plate Armor +1
**Senses** Passive Perception 13
**Languages** Common, Giant
**CR** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)

#### Traits

***Powerful Build.*** Gaurok has Advantage on any saving throw he makes to end the Grappled condition, and he counts as one size larger when determining his carrying capacity.

#### Actions

***Multiattack.*** Gaurok makes two Halberd attacks.

***Halberd +1.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +9, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 11 (1d10 + 6) Slashing damage. If the target is Large or smaller, Gaurok can give it the Prone condition (*Hill's Tumble*; 3/Day).

***Javelin.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +8, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d6 + 5) Piercing damage, and the target's Speed decreases by 10 feet until the start of Gaurok's next turn.

***Action Surge (1/Day).*** Gaurok makes two Halberd attacks in addition to his other actions this turn.

#### Bonus Actions

***Pole Strike.*** Immediately after making a Halberd attack, Gaurok makes one more: *Melee Attack Roll:* +9, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d4 + 6) Bludgeoning damage.

***Large Form (1/Day).*** For 10 minutes, Gaurok becomes Large, has Advantage on Strength checks, and his Speed increases by 10 feet.

***Second Wind (2/Day).*** Gaurok regains 14 (1d10 + 9) Hit Points.

***Misty Step (2/Day).*** Gaurok casts *Misty Step*, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability.

#### Reactions

***Sentinel.*** *Trigger:* A creature enters Gaurok's reach, or a creature within 5 feet of him takes the Disengage action or hits a target other than Gaurok. *Response:* Gaurok makes one Halberd attack against it. On a hit, the creature's Speed becomes 0 for the rest of the current turn.

***Shield (3/Day).*** *Trigger:* Gaurok is hit by an attack roll or targeted by *Magic Missile*. *Response:* Gaurok casts *Shield*, gaining a +5 bonus to AC until the start of his next turn, including against the triggering attack.

**Habitat:** Coastal, Urban (Calithra, Ravonia; Surfwale, Karsovia) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Treasure:** Armaments

## CR Doğrulaması

```
D-CR: HP 75, Shield ×1.1 → 82.5 → tablo CR 4 (84); AC 19 vs beklenen 14 → +5 → +2 CR = D-CR 6
O-CR: 3 tur DPR
      R1 Action Surge: 4 × Halberd 11 + Pole Strike 8                           = 52
      R2 2 × Halberd 11 + Pole Strike 8 + Sentinel 11 × %50 (koşullu)          = 35.5
      R3 aynı                                                                    = 35.5
      (52 + 35.5 + 35.5) / 3 = 41 → tablo CR 6 (41); attack +9 vs beklenen +7 → +2 → +1 CR = O-CR 7
CR = (6 + 7) / 2 = 6.5 → 6   [hedef: 6 — seviye 9 fighter, cr-math §7; ±1 içinde]   ✅
```

**Notlar:**
- *Plate Armor* +1 → AC 19; *Halberd* +1 → attack +9 (5 + 3 + 1), damage +6.
- *Hill's Tumble* (Prone) ve *Sentinel* (Speed 0) DPR'ye girmiyor — ama Uykusuzlar'ın esir alma planında asıl iş bunlarda: Prone + Paralyzed hedefe Aurel'in smite'ı.
- *Second Wind* iyileştirmesi D-CR'ye eklenmedi (2 × 14 = 28 HP ekstra; Uykusuzlar'ın gerçek dayanıklılığı yazılı HP'den yüksek).
