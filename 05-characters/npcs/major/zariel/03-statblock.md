# Zariel — Statblock

**Yukarı:** [Zariel](README.md)

> Statblock İngilizce tutulur — masada okunacak metin budur.
> Kaynak: *Baldur's Gate: Descent into Avernus* (2019).

---

**Large fiend (devil), lawful evil**

**Armor Class** 21 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 580 (40d10 + 360)
**Speed** 50 ft., fly 150 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 27 (+8) | 24 (+7) | 28 (+9) | 26 (+8) | 27 (+8) | 30 (+10) |

**Saving Throws** Dex +15, Con +17, Wis +16, Cha +18
**Skills** Insight +16, Perception +16, Persuasion +18
**Damage Resistances** cold; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
**Damage Immunities** fire, poison
**Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, poisoned
**Senses** truesight 120 ft., passive Perception 26
**Languages** all, telepathy 120 ft.
**Challenge** 26 (90,000 XP) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Proficiency Bonus** +8

---

### Traits

***Devil's Sight.*** Magical darkness doesn't impede Zariel's darkvision.

***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If Zariel fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.

***Magic Resistance.*** Zariel has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Magic Weapons.*** Zariel's weapon attacks are magical.

***Regeneration.*** Zariel regains 20 hit points at the start of her turn. If she takes radiant damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of her next turn. She dies only if she starts her turn with 0 hit points and doesn't regenerate.

***Innate Spellcasting.*** Zariel's spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 26). She can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:

At will: *detect evil and good*, *detect magic*, *fireball*, *invisibility* (self only), *wall of fire*
3/day each: *blade barrier*, *dispel evil and good*, *finger of death*
1/day each: *flame strike*, *plane shift*

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** Zariel makes three attacks: one with her Horrid Touch and two with her weapon.

***Horrid Touch.*** *Melee Spell Attack:* +18 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 22 (4d10) psychic damage, and the target is cursed for 1 minute. Until the curse ends, the target has disadvantage on saving throws against Zariel's spells.

***Flail of Zariel.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +16 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 8) bludgeoning damage plus 22 (4d10) fire damage.

***Longsword of Zariel.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +16 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 17 (2d10 + 8) slashing damage plus 22 (4d10) radiant damage.

***Teleport.*** Zariel magically teleports, along with any equipment she is wearing or carrying, up to 120 feet to an unoccupied space she can see.

### Bonus Actions

***Change Shape.*** Zariel magically transforms into a Medium or Large humanoid, or back into her true form. Her statistics are the same in each form, except for her speed. Any equipment she is wearing or carrying transforms with her.

### Legendary Actions

Zariel can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Zariel regains spent legendary actions at the start of her turn.

***Attack.*** Zariel makes one weapon attack.

***Teleport.*** Zariel uses her Teleport action.

***Burning Gaze (Costs 2 Actions).*** Zariel targets one creature she can see within 120 feet of her. The target must succeed on a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw or take 22 (4d10) fire damage and catch fire, taking 11 (2d10) fire damage at the start of each of its turns until a creature uses an action to extinguish the flames.

## Lair Actions (Avernus)

Avernus'ta savaşırken, initiative count 20'de (kaybeden beraberlik) Zariel şunlardan
birini kullanabilir:

- **Blood Rain.** 60 ft yarıçapındaki alan kan yağmuruyla dolar; alandaki düşmanlar
  DC 20 Constitution save yapar, başarısızlıkta bir sonraki turlarına kadar
  saldırı zarlarında dezavantaj.
- **Infernal Reinforcements.** 1d4 **spined devil** ya da 1 **bone devil** Zariel'in
  gördüğü boş bir alana belirir; onun initiative'inde hareket eder.
- **The War Answers.** Yakındaki bir savaş enkazı canlanır; 30 ft çapında bir alan
  difficult terrain olur ve içindekiler 2d6 slashing damage alır.

## Taktikler

**Zariel bir komutan gibi savaşır, bir canavar gibi değil.**

| Öncelik | Ne yapar |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Destek/healer'ı önce hedefler.** Savaşı uzatan kişiyi ilk çıkarır. |
| 2 | **Horrid Touch'ı en tehlikeli caster'a.** Save dezavantajı sonraki büyüleri katlar. |
| 3 | **Havada kalır** (fly 150 ft). Melee sınıfları ona ulaşamazsa savaş biter. |
| 4 | **Teleport ile pozisyon alır**, kaçmaz. |
| 5 | **Radiant damage alırsa dikkatli olur** — regeneration'ı kesen tek şey odur. |

**Ne zaman durur:** Zariel gereksiz risk almaz. Savaşı kaybettiğini anlarsa
**pazarlığa açılır** — kaçmaz, teslim olmaz, **teklif eder**.

Bu, masadaki en güçlü an olabilir: bir CR 26 archdevil, savaşı durdurup
partiye bir anlaşma önerir.
