### Sylvaine Orrec

*Medium Humanoid (Elf, Wizard), Lawful Neutral*

**AC** 15 &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; **Initiative** +1 (11)
**HP** 75 (10d8 + 30)
**Speed** 30 ft.

| | MOD | SAVE | | MOD | SAVE | | MOD | SAVE |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|:---:|:---:|---|:---:|:---:|
| **Str** 8 | −1 | +0 | **Dex** 13 | +1 | +2 | **Con** 16 | +3 | +4 |
| **Int** 20 | +5 | +9 | **Wis** 12 | +1 | +5 | **Cha** 10 | +0 | +1 |

**Skills** Arcana +11, History +8, Investigation +8, Perception +4, Religion +8
**Gear** Cloak of Protection, Pearl of Power, Spellbook, Wand of the War Mage +1
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 14
**Languages** Common, Draconic, Elvish
**CR** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)

#### Traits

***Fey Ancestry.*** Sylvaine has Advantage on saving throws she makes to avoid or end the Charmed condition.

***Portent (2/Day).*** At dawn, Sylvaine rolls two d20s and records the numbers. Before a creature she can see makes a D20 Test, she can replace the roll with one of these numbers.

#### Actions

***Multiattack.*** Sylvaine makes three Arcane Burst attacks.

***Arcane Burst.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +9, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Force damage.

***Spellcasting.*** Sylvaine casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16):

**At Will:** *Detect Magic*, *Mage Armor* (included in AC), *Message*, *Minor Illusion*, *Prestidigitation*

**2/Day Each:** *Fireball* (level 4 version), *Hold Person* (level 3 version), *Slow*, *Web*

**1/Day Each:** *Arcane Eye*, *Banishment*, *Greater Invisibility*, *Hold Monster*, *Hypnotic Pattern*, *See Invisibility* (cast before combat), *Wall of Force*

#### Bonus Actions

***Misty Step (2/Day).*** Sylvaine casts *Misty Step*, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.

#### Reactions

***Protective Magic (3/Day).*** Sylvaine casts *Counterspell* or *Shield* in response to that spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.

**Habitat:** Coastal, Urban (Calithra, Ravonia; Surfwale, Karsovia) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Treasure:** Arcana

## CR Doğrulaması

```
D-CR: HP 75, Protective Magic (Shield) ×1.1 → 82.5 → tablo CR 4 (84); AC 15 vs beklenen 14 → fark 1, düzeltme yok = D-CR 4
O-CR: 3 tur DPR
      R1 Fireball (level 4, 9d6 = 31) × 2 hedef (AoE ×2)          = 62
      R2 Fireball (level 4) × 2 hedef                              = 62
      R3 3 × Arcane Burst 14                                       = 42
      (62 + 62 + 42) / 3 = 55.3 → tablo CR 8 (53); DC 16 vs beklenen 15 → fark 1, düzeltme yok = O-CR 8
CR = (4 + 8) / 2 = 6   [hedef: 6 — seviye 9 wizard, cr-math §7]   ✅
```

**Notlar:**
- SRD **Mage** deseni (cr-math §5): cam top — düşük D-CR, yüksek O-CR.
- *Wand of the War Mage* +1 → Arcane Burst attack **+9** (5 + 3 + 1). *Cloak of Protection* +1 → AC ve **bütün save'ler.**
- Gerçek tehlike DPR değil, **kontrol**: *Hold Person*, *Wall of Force*, *Hold Monster*, *Counterspell*. Uykusuzlar'ın esir alma planı Sylvaine'in kilitlerine dayanıyor; bu, CR tablosunun ölçmediği şey.
