### Ser Aurel Drevka

*Medium Humanoid (Aasimar, Paladin), Lawful Good*

**AC** 20 &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; **Initiative** +0 (10)
**HP** 78 (12d8 + 24)
**Speed** 30 ft.

| **Int** 8 | −1 | −1 | **Wis** 12 | +1 | +4 | **Cha** 16 | +3 | +6 |

**Skills** Athletics +7, Insight +4, Intimidation +6, Perception +4, Persuasion +6
**Resistances** Necrotic, Radiant
**Immunities** Charmed
**Gear** Dimensional Shackles, Javelins (4), Longsword +1, Manacles (2), Plate Armor, Sentinel Shield
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 14
**Languages** Common, Elvish
**CR** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)

#### Traits

***Aura of Protection.*** While Aurel doesn't have the Incapacitated condition, he and his allies within 10 feet of him add 3 to their saving throws, and they can't be Charmed.

***Sentinel Shield.*** Aurel has Advantage on Initiative rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks.

#### Actions

***Multiattack.*** Aurel makes two Longsword attacks. Once per turn, when a Longsword attack hits, he can also use Shield Bash.

***Longsword +1.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d8 + 5) Slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) Radiant damage.

***Shield Bash.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 15, the creature Aurel just hit with a Longsword attack. *Failure:* Aurel either pushes the target 5 feet straight away from him or gives it the Prone condition.

***Javelin.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +7, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Piercing damage, and the target's Speed decreases by 10 feet until the start of Aurel's next turn.

***Abjure Foes (1/Day).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 14, up to three creatures Aurel can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition for 1 minute or until it takes damage. While Frightened, it can move, take an action, or take a Bonus Action on its turn, but only one of them.

***Spellcasting.*** Aurel casts one of the following spells, using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):

**1/Day Each:** *Aid*, *Bless*, *Command*, *Dispel Magic*, *Protection from Evil and Good*, *Revivify*, *Zone of Truth*

#### Bonus Actions

***Divine Smite (2/Day).*** Immediately after Aurel hits a target with a melee attack, the target takes an extra 13 (3d8) Radiant damage, or 18 (4d8) Radiant damage if it's a Fiend or an Undead.

***Lay On Hands.*** Aurel touches a creature and restores up to 50 Hit Points to it, drawn from a pool of 50 that refills when he finishes a Long Rest.

***Heavenly Wings (1/Day).*** For 1 minute, ash-grey spectral wings give Aurel a Fly Speed of 30 feet, and once on each of his turns, one target he damages takes an extra 3 Radiant damage.

#### Reactions

***Interpose Shield.*** *Trigger:* Aurel is subjected to an effect that allows him to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage. *Response:* If Aurel succeeds on the save, he takes no damage.

**Habitat:** Coastal, Urban (Calithra, Ravonia; Surfwale, Karsovia) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Treasure:** Armaments

## CR Doğrulaması

```
D-CR: HP 78, Resistance 2 tip ×1.1 → 86 → tablo CR 4 (84); AC 20 vs beklenen 14 → +6 → +3 CR = D-CR 7
O-CR: 3 tur DPR
      Her tur: 2 × Longsword (9 + 4 = 13) = 26 + Heavenly Wings 3 = 29
      + Divine Smite 2/Day: 2 × 13 = 26, 3 tura bölünür                        = +8.7
      29 + 8.7 = 37.7 → tablo CR 5 (35); attack +8 vs beklenen +7 → fark 1, düzeltme yok = O-CR 5
CR = (7 + 5) / 2 = 6   [hedef: 6 — seviye 9 paladin, cr-math §7]   ✅
```

**Notlar:**
- *Longsword* +1 attack ve damage'a işlendi (4 + 3 + 1 = +8; 1d8 + 4 + 1).
- Radiant rider (1d8) = *Sacred Weapon* + yemin ışığı; Knight deseni (cr-math §4). Yüksek AC (20) yüzünden HP ve smite sayısı düşük tutuldu — "vurulması zor ama devrilince biter".
- Aura of Protection effective HP'ye **girmedi** (kendi save'leri için ×1.05, yuvarlanınca değişmiyor) — ama dostlarına verdiği +3, Uykusuzlar'ı birlikte **bir CR** daha sert yapar.
- *Dimensional Shackles* savaş eşyası değil; CR'ye girmez.
