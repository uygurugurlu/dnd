# The Forgotten Realms: A Complete History

The Forgotten Realms is one of D&D's oldest and deepest campaign settings, created by Ed Greenwood in the late 1960s and published by TSR/Wizards of the Coast starting in 1987. Its history spans cosmic creation through the present "era" of 5th edition. Here's the comprehensive breakdown.

## Cosmic Origins: Before Time

In the beginning there was only the Void, from which emerged **Lord Ao**, the Overgod. Ao is not worshipped and takes no sides; he is the overseer who ensures the gods fulfill their duties to mortals. From the primordial chaos, two twin goddesses emerged: [Selûne](../pantheon/faerunian/selune.md) (light, moon, good) and [Shar](../pantheon/faerunian/shar.md) (darkness, loss, secrets). They are sisters and eternal enemies, and their conflict shaped the cosmos.

Together they created **Chauntea** (the earth/life goddess, embodiment of Abeir-Toril itself). When Shar tried to destroy Chauntea's new world, Selûne ignited the sun by tearing fire from her own essence. Enraged, Shar struck her, and from Selûne's wound came **Mystryl**, goddess of magic — the first incarnation of the Weave. This established the fundamental balance: light and dark, magic woven between them.

The primordials and early gods then warred for control of the world Abeir-Toril. Eventually the world was split in two by divine decree — **Abeir** (ruled by primordials and dragons) and **Toril** (ruled by gods and their mortal followers). This split becomes important much later during the Spellplague.

## The Days of Thunder (−35,000 to −30,000 DR)

The earliest age of Toril, dominated by the **creator races**: sarrukh (serpentfolk), batrachi (frog-folk), aearee (bird-folk), and the LeShay. This was also the age of the **dragons** and **giants**, whose empires would follow. Mortals barely existed. The sarrukh empire of Isstosseffifil and others rose and fell. It ended with the **Tearfall**, when a rain of fiery stones devastated the world and shattered the batrachi empire.

## The Age of Giants and Dragons (−30,000 to −24,000 DR)

Giants (the empire of **Ostoria**) and dragons warred endlessly. This is when the god **Io** (Asgorath) scattered dragonkind, and giant mythology (Annam All-Father) took shape. Elves had arrived via portal from the plane of Faerie, beginning elven history on Toril.

## The First Flowering / Elven Empires (−24,000 to −12,000 DR)

The great elven civilizations rose: **Aryvandaar**, **Illefarn**, **Miyeritar**, **Keltormir**, **Shantel Othreier**, and others. This was the golden age of the elves. Dwarves (children of **Moradin**) also established their early kingdoms under the mountains.

Then came the **Crown Wars** (−12,000 to −9,000 DR), five devastating wars among the elven nations sparked by Aryvandaar's imperialism. The dark elves of the Ilythiiri were corrupted by worship of [Lolth](../pantheon/racial/lolth.md) (formerly Araushnee, consort of Corellon Larethian, who betrayed him). At the end of the Fourth Crown War, the good gods of the elven pantheon **transformed the Ilythiiri into the drow** and banished them underground. This is the origin of the drow and the split of the elven race.

During or after this, the surviving elves performed the **Sundering** (the first one) — a mass ritual that raised the island continent of **Evermeet** from the sea as a refuge.

## Rise of the Younger Races (−9,000 to −3,000 DR)

Humans, who had lived as scattered tribes, began to rise. The dwarven kingdom of **Shanatar** flourished. In the south, the **Imaskari** — human wizards of extraordinary power — built an empire and, to solve a plague, opened a portal to another world and enslaved humans from it (these would become the **Mulan**). When the Imaskari blocked the slaves' gods from reaching them, those gods incarnated as mortals and became the **Mulhorandi pantheon** — Horus, Set, Osiris, Isis, Anubis, etc. They overthrew the Imaskari in −2488 DR, founding **Mulhorand** and **Unther**, the oldest continuous human nations on Faerûn.

## Netheril and the Age of Magic (−3,859 to −339 DR)

Perhaps the most important pre-modern era. **Netheril** was a human empire in the north that achieved unimaginable magical heights. Its archwizards created **mythallars** — devices that powered entire floating cities in the sky. Names like **Karsus**, **Ioulaum**, and **Shadow** became legend.

**Karsus's Folly (−339 DR)** is arguably the most catastrophic event in Realms history before the Spellplague. The archwizard Karsus cast **Karsus's Avatar**, a 12th-level spell designed to let him *become* the god of magic and seize Mystryl's portfolio. It worked — for a single moment. Karsus briefly became a god, but he could not handle the flood of magic flowing through him, and Mystryl was forced to sacrifice herself to save the Weave from collapse. She reincarnated immediately as [Mystra](../pantheon/faerunian/mystra.md) (first incarnation), but the Weave was weakened, Netheril's floating cities crashed, and Karsus was turned to stone. Netheril fell in a single day. The **phaerimm**, ancient enemies of Netheril, then drained the region and created the **Anauroch desert**. The surviving Netherese shadow mages became the **Shadovar** of Thultanthar, who fled into the Plane of Shadow.

Other significant civilizations rose in this era: **Jhaamdath** (destroyed by elven tsunami), **Calimshan** (founded by genies), **Chondath**, and the elven realm of **Cormanthyr** (which would become Myth Drannor).

## The Founding Era (−339 to 1 DR)

Human nations proliferated across Faerûn. **Cormanthyr** built the great city of Myth Drannor where all races could live together. Human kingdoms like **Chessenta**, **Impiltur**, and early **Cormyr** (founded ~26 DR) emerged. **Dalereckoning (DR)** — the standard calendar — was established in the Year of Sunrise (1 DR), when the Dalesmen were granted the right to settle the forests by the elves of Cormanthor.

## Dalereckoning: The Long Middle Period (1 DR – 1300 DR)

This is the era of legendary heroes and steadily rising and falling kingdoms. Key events:

- **The Fall of Myth Drannor (714 DR)** — a coalition of demons (the Army of Darkness) overwhelms the greatest elven-human city. The elves begin their long **Retreat** to Evermeet.
- **Rise of Waterdeep** as the City of Splendors, centered on Mount Waterdeep where dwarves once mined.
- The **Mulhorandi** and **Untherite** empires stagnate but endure.
- **Thay** breaks away from Mulhorand (922 DR), becoming the infamous magocracy of the Red Wizards.
- The **Iron Throne**, the [Zhentarim](../../06-campaigns/chains-of-the-burning-compact/factions-in-play/zhentarim-ravonia.md), the **Harpers**, and other long-running organizations form.

## The Time of Troubles / Godswar (1358 DR)

This is the setting's defining modern event and the boundary between 1e/2e Forgotten Realms. The gods [Bane](../pantheon/faerunian/bane.md) and **Myrkul** (lord of the dead) stole the **Tablets of Fate** from Lord Ao — tablets that recorded each god's responsibilities. Furious, Ao cast every deity out of the planes and forced them to walk Toril as **mortal avatars** until the tablets were recovered. The Weave faltered; magic became wild and unreliable.

During the Time of Troubles:

- [Mystra](../pantheon/faerunian/mystra.md) (1st) was killed by [Helm](../pantheon/faerunian/helm.md) at the Celestial Stairway on Ao's orders. A young mortal sorceress, **Midnight**, was chosen by Ao to ascend and become the new [Mystra](../pantheon/faerunian/mystra.md) (2nd).
- [Bane](../pantheon/faerunian/bane.md) was killed by [Torm](../pantheon/faerunian/torm.md) (both died, but Torm was resurrected).
- **Myrkul** was destroyed by Midnight over Waterdeep.
- **Bhaal**, god of murder, was killed by the mortal **Cyric**, who took Bhaal's portfolio — but not before Bhaal sired mortal children (the **Bhaalspawn**, basis of the *Baldur's Gate* video games).
- **Cyric** also killed **Leira** (goddess of illusion) and took her portfolio, becoming a new major evil god.
- [Kelemvor](../pantheon/faerunian/kelemvor.md) (a mortal adventurer) eventually became the new god of the dead, replacing Myrkul.
- [Helm](../pantheon/faerunian/helm.md) became disgraced for killing Mystra while "just following orders."

This is when many of the "current" gods of the 2e/3e Realms took their thrones. It reshuffled the pantheon more than any event in history.

## The Era of Upheaval (1358 – 1385 DR)

This is the classic 2e and 3e period. Cyric becomes increasingly mad. The Zhentarim rise. The drow of Menzoberranzan (and **Drizzt Do'Urden**) enter the spotlight. The **Return of the Archwizards** sees the Shadovar emerge from the Plane of Shadow, reclaiming the city of **Shade** and rising over Anauroch. The **Chosen of Mystra** (Elminster, the Seven Sisters, Khelben Blackstaff) are at the height of their power. It is the heroic high-fantasy "default" Realms most people picture.

## The Spellplague (1385 DR) — The Second Worldshaking Event

**Cyric**, manipulated by [Shar](../pantheon/faerunian/shar.md), **murdered Mystra** in her own domain. With the god of magic dead, the **Weave collapsed**. Magic convulsed across the world in blue fire, killing millions, reshaping continents, and tearing the veil between Abeir and Toril. Parts of each world swapped places — chunks of the primordial-ruled Abeir appeared on Toril and vice versa. The **Sea of Fallen Stars** partially drained. **Halruaa** was annihilated. **Unther** was almost entirely replaced by a chunk of Abeir and its surviving god **Gilgeam** was killed. New lands like **Returned Abeir** appeared. The calendar advanced ~100 years to around **1479 DR** as the "current" default.

This was the 4th Edition Realms — deeply controversial among fans because it radically changed the map, killed beloved characters, and shifted the tone. Gods like [Mystra](../pantheon/faerunian/mystra.md) were dead; [Asmodeus](../pantheon/archdevils/asmodeus.md) ascended from archdevil to full god by absorbing Azuth; the setting felt post-apocalyptic.

## The Second Sundering (1482 – 1487 DR) — The Reset

In response to fan reaction, 5th Edition introduced the **Second Sundering**. The **Chosen** of various gods arose once more, and **Ao** himself intervened. Abeir and Toril were separated again, geography partly restored, **Mystra returned** (with a resurrected Weave), and many pre-Spellplague gods came back. The Sundering allowed old fan-favorites to return without completely erasing 4e events. The current default year for 5e Realms is around **1489–1496 DR**, the **Era of Upheaval continued / Era of Awakening**.

## Key Deities by Era (Simplified)

**Creation/Dawn:** Ao (overgod), Selûne, Shar, Chauntea, Mystryl.

**Days of Thunder / Giant-Dragon Age:** Io, Annam, Asgorath, Bahamut, Tiamat dominant.

**Elven Empires:** Corellon Larethian, Sehanine, Araushnee (→ Lolth), Moradin for dwarves.

**Netheril Era:** **Mystryl → Mystra (1st)** is the defining deity. Amaunator (sun god, will "die" and return as Lathander, then become Amaunator again post-Spellplague). Jergal is the original god of death (voluntarily splits his portfolio among Bane, Bhaal, and Myrkul in −358 DR).

**Dalereckoning Middle Period:** The classic Faerûnian pantheon solidifies — Tyr, Torm, Ilmater, Lathander, Tempus, Mielikki, Silvanus, Oghma, Mystra, Selûne, Sune, Waukeen (good/neutral); Bane, Bhaal, Myrkul, Cyric, Shar, Talos, Malar, Lolth, Talona, Loviatar, Umberlee (evil); plus Helm, Kelemvor, Mask, etc.

**Time of Troubles & After:** Bane (killed, later returns), Myrkul (killed, replaced by Kelemvor), Bhaal (killed, returns later), Mystra 2 (Midnight), Cyric (new evil god), Iyachtu Xvim (Bane's son who later becomes Bane again).

**Spellplague Era (4e):** Mystra dead, [Asmodeus](../pantheon/archdevils/asmodeus.md) ascends to full god, Amaunator replaces Lathander as the sun god's "true" form, Bhaal & Bane return through Bhaalspawn and Xvim respectively. Cyric imprisoned after going too far.

**Post-Sundering (5e):** Mystra restored, Weave healed, most classic gods back in place. Shar is the primary cosmic antagonist working through schemes rather than open war.

## Recurring Themes and Notes

The defining conflict of the Realms is **Selûne vs. Shar** — the eternal struggle of light and dark, memory and oblivion — which underlies nearly every cosmic event. **Mystra and the Weave** are the linchpin of reality; whenever the god of magic dies, catastrophe follows (Karsus, the Time of Troubles, the Spellplague). **Ao** stays aloof but intervenes exactly three times in recorded history to reset the divine order: creating the rule that gods need worshippers, the Time of Troubles, and the Second Sundering.

The Realms deliberately layer civilizations on top of ruins — Netheril's fall underlies the Anauroch and the Shadovar; the elven empires underlie Cormanthor and Myth Drannor; the Imaskari underlie Mulhorand and Thay. Almost any dungeon can be justified as "something old and terrible lies beneath."
