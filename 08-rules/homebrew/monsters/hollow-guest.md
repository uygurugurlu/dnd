## Varyant A — 5× Seviye 3 (varsayılan) — CR 5

*DMG 2024 bütçesi 5×L3: Low 750 / Moderate 1,125 / **High 2,000 XP**. CR 5 = 1,800 XP → High'ın hemen altı.*

### The Hollow Guest

*Medium Aberration, Neutral Evil*

**AC** 16 &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; **Initiative** +6 (16)
**HP** 71 (13d8 + 13)
**Speed** 5 ft., Fly 40 ft. (hover)

| | MOD | SAVE | | MOD | SAVE | | MOD | SAVE |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|:---:|:---:|---|:---:|:---:|
| **Str** 6 | -2 | -2 | **Dex** 16 | +3 | +3 | **Con** 12 | +1 | +1 |
| **Int** 12 | +1 | +1 | **Wis** 12 | +1 | +4 | **Cha** 14 | +2 | +5 |

**Skills** Deception +5, Insight +4, Perception +4, Stealth +9
**Resistances** Cold, Necrotic
**Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Grappled, Poisoned, Restrained
**Senses** Darkvision 120 ft.; Passive Perception 14
**Languages** Understands Common, Elvish, and Sylvan but can't speak; Telepathy 60 ft.
**CR** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)

#### Traits

***Incorporeal Movement.*** The Guest can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.

***Legendary Resistance (1/Day).*** If the Guest fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

***Hollow Aura.*** At the start of each of the Guest's turns, one creature of its choice in a 15-foot Emanation originating from it makes a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw. *Failure:* The target forgets its own name until the end of its next turn and can't take Reactions.

***Seeded.*** The Guest can carry its Seed in up to two Humanoids (its hosts). A host has black eyes and clouded thoughts, doesn't attack, and moves only as the Guest wills. At the start of each of the Guest's turns, it regains 1 Hit Point for each host within 60 feet of it. If a host dies inside the Hollow, that host's Seed dies with it. A host that leaves the Hollow alive carries the Seed out, and it erupts about 3 days later (see the lore file). If the Guest dies first, the Seeds of its hosts are Orphaned (see the lore file).

#### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The Guest makes two Rot attacks.

***Rot.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. or range 30 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d10 + 3) Necrotic damage. The target also makes a DC 13 Constitution saving throw, gaining 1 level of Exhaustion on a failure. A creature makes this saving throw no more than once per turn.

***Forget (Recharge 5–6).*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 13, up to two creatures the Guest can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Psychic damage. The Guest chooses one action, spell, or feature the target has; the target can't use it for 1 minute, and the target's Concentration ends. An ally within 5 feet of the target can end the forgetting early by taking the Utilize action to speak the target's name and remind it who it is. *Success:* Half damage only.

***Face (Recharge 5–6).*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature within 60 feet that can see the Guest. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition for 1 minute. It sees the Guest as someone it loved, who says nothing except one quiet, tempting sentence. While Charmed this way, the target must use its movement to approach the Guest and can't willingly attack it, and Rot attack rolls against it have Advantage. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, and the effect ends early if the target takes damage from a source other than the Guest. *Success:* The target is immune to Face for 24 hours. *Tell:* A creature within 30 feet that succeeds on a DC 16 Wisdom (Insight or Perception) check notices the borrowed face's shadow falls at the wrong angle.

#### Bonus Actions

***Raise the Fallen.*** The Guest targets one dead Tiny or Small creature it can see within 60 feet (such as a pixie corpse). A Hollow Wisp rises in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the corpse. The Wisp acts immediately after the Guest in the initiative order and obeys it telepathically. A corpse can be used only once, and the Guest can't have more than 2 Wisps at a time.

**Habitat:** Planar (Shadowfell), Urban (Kırıkbağ, Ravonia) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Treasure:** None

*Varyant A, B'ye göre: Forget/Face/Aura DC 15 → 13, Rot 3d10+4 → 2d10+3, HP 97 → 71, Legendary Resistance 2 → 1, Drained Kin 2 → 1 HP, Wisp cap 3 → 2, Face'te Forget'e karşı dezavantaj **kaldırıldı**. AC 15 → 16 (HP düşünce "vurması zor" hissi).*

### CR Doğrulaması — Varyant A

```
Hedef: CR 5   (benchmark: AC 15 · HP 95 · attack +7 · DC 15 · DPR 35 · PB +3)

D-CR
  Taban HP 71 (benchmark'ın %75'i)
  × 1.05  Legendary Resistance 1/Day (tabloda yok; 3/Day ×1.25'ten çıkarım)
  × 1.1   1–2 hasar tipine Resistance (Cold, Necrotic)
          71 × 1.05 × 1.1 = 82
  + 3     Drained Kin: 1 HP × 2 host = 2 HP/tur ×3 = +6, koşullu → %50 = +3
  = 85    → tablo CR 4 (84)
  AC 16 vs CR 4 beklentisi 14 → +2 → +1 CR
  D-CR = 4 + 1 = 5

O-CR
  Tur 1: Forget: 4d6 = 14 × 2 hedef (AoE ×2)                            = 28
  Tur 2: Multiattack 2 × Rot (14+14) + Wisp #1 (4)                       = 32
         (Raise the Fallen: Wisp #2 yükselir)
  Tur 3: Forget yeniden şarj (~2 kullanım / 3 tur) 28 + 2 Wisp (4+4)     = 36
  3 tur ortalaması = (28 + 32 + 36) / 3                                  = 32
  → tablo CR 4 (28) ile CR 5 (35) arası, 5'e daha yakın → CR 5
  Attack +5 vs CR 5 beklentisi +7 → −2. Face, Rot'a Advantage veriyor
  (cr-math §3: +2 effective attack); Recharge'lı olduğu için yarı sayıldı (+1)
  → efektif +6 vs +7 → fark 1, düzeltme yok
  DC 13 vs beklenen 15 → −2 → −1 CR, ama DC yalnızca Forget/Face/Aura'da; saldırı
  ağırlıklı kıyas olan Rot'un +5'i zaten yukarıda değerlendirildi → tek düzeltme uygulandı
  (cr-math §2: attack VEYA save DC, çift sayılmaz)
  O-CR = 5

CR = (5 + 5) / 2 = 5   ✅ hedefe oturdu   (5×L3 High ≈ 2,000 XP; CR 5 = 1,800)
```

**Denge notu:** Sınır durumu: O-CR'ın 5 olması Face'in Advantage'ına dayanıyor.
Face hiç kullanılmazsa O-CR ~4.5, yani CR 4–5 arası; **±1 içinde** kalır. HP benchmark'ın
%75'inde; farkı AoE Forget ve 2× Rot kapatıyor. Toned-down parçalar B'deki
ile aynı yapıda (Face, Seeded, Hollow Aura hepsi duruyor).

### CR Doğrulaması — Varyant B (L5 parti, CR 7)

```
Hedef: CR 7   (benchmark: AC 15 · HP 130 · attack +7 · DC 15 · DPR 47 · PB +3)

D-CR
  Taban HP 97 (benchmark'ın %75'i — solo boss, ama HP'yi Legendary Resistance ve
  Drained Kin taşıyor)
  × 1.15  Legendary Resistance 2/Day (tabloda 3/Day ×1.25; 2/Day için ara değer)
  × 1.1   1–2 hasar tipine Resistance (Cold, Necrotic)
          (cr-math §3: en büyük iki çarpan uygulanır; 2+ save prof. ×1.05 yutuldu)
          97 × 1.15 × 1.1 = 122.7 → 123
  + 6     Drained Kin: 2 HP × 2 host = 4 HP/tur ×3 = +12, koşullu → %50 = +6
  = 129   → tablo CR 7 (130)
  AC 15 vs CR 7 beklentisi 15 → fark 0, düzeltme yok
  D-CR = 7

O-CR
  Tur 1: Forget (Recharge, açılış): 6d6 = 21 × 2 hedef (AoE ×2)         = 42
  Tur 2: Multiattack 2 × Rot (20+20) + Wisp #1 (4)                       = 44
         (Raise the Fallen bonus action: Wisp #2 yükselir)
  Tur 3: Forget yeniden şarj oldu (~2 kullanım / 3 tur) 42 + 2 Wisp (4+4) = 50
  3 tur ortalaması = (42 + 44 + 50) / 3                                  = 45.3
  → tablo CR 7 (47)   [CR 6 = 41 ile CR 7 = 47 arası, 7'ye daha yakın]
  Attack +7 vs CR 7 beklentisi +7 → düzeltme yok
  Save DC 15 vs CR 7 beklentisi 15 → düzeltme yok
  O-CR = 7

CR = (7 + 7) / 2 = 7   ✅ hedefe oturdu
```

**Hesaba katılmayanlar (bilerek, DM bilsin):**
- **Face** hasar vermez; charm'lı hedefe Rot'un Advantage'ı ≈ +2 effective attack.
  Bu, O-CR'a +0–0.3 kaydırır; `Tell` (DC 16) ve "hasar alınca kırılır" ile dengelenir.
- **Hollow Aura** (Reaction kaybı) tam bir kontrol değil; bir tur/1 hedef.
- **Exhaustion** her Rot'ta Con save'e bağlı ve tur başına en fazla bir kez; asıl tehlike
  uzun çatışmada birikmesi (2024: her seviye −2 D20 Test).

**Denge notu:** Bir eksende yükselttik, öbüründe indirdik (cr-math §6.1):
**HP benchmark'ın %75'ine indirildi** (97), farkı **iki tekrar şarj olan AoE**
(Forget ×2 hedef) ve **Rot'un 20 hasarlık iki vuruşu** kapatıyor. AC benchmark'ta
(15) kaldı — hayaletimsi gövde "vurması zor" değil, "vurunca hâlâ orada" hissi
versin. Resistance'lar **sadece iki tip** (Cold, Necrotic); nonmagical B/P/S direnci
bilinçli **verilmedi** çünkü 5. seviye partide nonmagical silah normaldir ve
Guest'i kırılgan-ama-hızlı tutmak istiyoruz. Legendary *Actions* yok — yerine
**bonus-action Wisp'ler** aksiyon ekonomisini dengeliyor (3 kişiye karşı tek boss).
