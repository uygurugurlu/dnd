### Broken Horn Raider

*Medium Humanoid (Orc), Chaotic Neutral*

**AC** 13 &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; **Initiative** +1 (11)
**HP** 22 (4d8 + 4)
**Speed** 30 ft.

|---|:---:|:---:|---|:---:|:---:|---|:---:|:---:|
| **Str** 16 | +3 | +3 | **Dex** 12 | +1 | +1 | **Con** 13 | +1 | +1 |
| **Int** 8 | −1 | −1 | **Wis** 11 | +0 | +0 | **Cha** 9 | −1 | −1 |

**Skills** Stealth +3, Survival +2
**Gear** Greataxe, Hide Armor, Javelins (3), Rope
**Senses** Darkvision 120 ft.; Passive Perception 10
**Languages** Common, Orc
**CR** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)

#### Traits

***Relentless Endurance (1/Day).*** When the raider drops to 0 Hit Points but isn't killed outright, it drops to 1 Hit Point instead.

#### Actions

***Greataxe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d12 + 3) Slashing damage. If the raider moved at least 20 feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 4 (1d8) Slashing damage.

***Javelin.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage.

***Snatch.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 13, one Medium or smaller creature within 5 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13). While grappling a Small or Medium creature this way, the raider's Speed isn't halved for moving it.

#### Bonus Actions

***Adrenaline Rush (2/Day).*** The raider takes the Dash action and gains 2 Temporary Hit Points.

**Habitat:** Forest, Grassland (Ravonia — Kırık Boynuz menzili) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Treasure:** Individual

## CR Doğrulaması

### Broken Horn Raider — hedef CR 1

```
Benchmark CR 1: AC 12 · HP 33 · attack +5 · DPR 12

D-CR
  HP 22 (benchmark'ın %67'si — humanoid NPC deseni)
  + ~5  Relentless Endurance (bir "fazladan vuruş")      → 27
  → tablo CR 1/2 (22) – CR 1 (33) arası → CR 1/2
  AC 13 vs 12 → fark 1, düzeltme yok
  D-CR = 1/2

O-CR
  Greataxe 6.5 + 3 = 9.5; hücum bonusu 4.5 × %50 (koşullu) = 2.25
  DPR ≈ 11.75 → tablo CR 1 (12). Attack +5 = beklenen
  O-CR = 1

CR = (0.5 + 1) / 2 = 0.75 → 1   ✅
```
