### Sealed Thrall

*Medium Humanoid (Possessed), Lawful Evil*

**AC** 12 &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; **Initiative** +1 (11)
**HP** 32 (5d8 + 10)
**Speed** 30 ft.

| **Str** 14 | +2 | +2 | **Dex** 12 | +1 | +1 | **Con** 14 | +2 | +2 |
| **Int** 6 | −2 | −2 | **Wis** 10 | +0 | +0 | **Cha** 8 | −1 | −1 |

**Resistances** Fire
**Gear** Leather Armor, Pitchfork
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 10
**Languages** Common, Infernal
**CR** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)

#### Traits

***Hostage Body.*** The thrall's body belongs to a living villager. When the thrall drops to 0 Hit Points, the devil inside is expelled to Avernus, and the creature that dealt the damage chooses whether the villager dies or falls Unconscious and stable.

***Infernal Tenant.*** The thrall counts as both a Humanoid and a Fiend for any effect that targets or detects creature types. If the thrall is targeted by *Remove Curse* or by the Dismissal option of *Dispel Evil and Good*, the devil is expelled and the villager falls Unconscious and stable.

***Sealed Chorus.*** When any Sealed creature within 1 mile of the thrall uses Infernal Shriek or Signal Horn, the thrall learns that creature's location.

#### Actions

***Pitchfork.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) Fire damage.

***Hurled Tool.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +4, range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Bludgeoning damage.

#### Bonus Actions

***Infernal Shriek (1/Day).*** The thrall shrieks in a voice that is not its own. The sound is audible out to 300 feet, and every Sealed creature within 1 mile learns the thrall's location.

**Habitat:** Grassland, Urban (Wheatrest, Ravonia) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Treasure:** Individual

  HP 32 × 1.1 (Fire resistance)                        → 35
  → tablo CR 1 (33). AC 12 vs 12 → düzeltme yok
