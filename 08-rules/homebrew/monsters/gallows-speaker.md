### Gallows Speaker

*Huge Undead, Chaotic Evil*

**AC** 14 &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; **Initiative** +2 (12)
**HP** 67 (9d12 + 9)
**Speed** 5 ft., Fly 40 ft. (hover)

| | MOD | SAVE | | MOD | SAVE | | MOD | SAVE |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|:---:|:---:|---|:---:|:---:|
| **Str** 9 | -1 | -1 | **Dex** 14 | +2 | +2 | **Con** 12 | +1 | +1 |
| **Int** 10 | +0 | +0 | **Wis** 12 | +1 | +1 | **Cha** 17 | +3 | +3 |

**Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing, Thunder
**Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
**Senses** Truesight 60 ft.; Passive Perception 11
**Languages** Common, Elvish, Sylvan
**CR** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)

#### Traits

***Legendary Resistance (1/Day).*** If the Speaker fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

***Whirling Form.*** The Speaker can enter another creature's space and stop there.

***Aura of Violence.*** At the end of each of the Speaker's turns, each creature of its choice in a 15-foot Emanation originating from the Speaker makes a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw. *Failure:* The target uses its Reaction to make a melee attack against a creature of the Speaker's choice within the target's reach. If no creature is within its reach, or it has no Reaction available, the target instead takes 7 (2d6) Psychic damage. The Aura is inactive while the Speaker is in a guise (see Borrowed Face).

***Kept Voices.*** The Speaker can carry its Voice in up to two Humanoid hosts. A host has black eyes and clouded thoughts, takes no actions, and moves only as the Speaker wills. A host that dies inside the Hollow takes its Voice with it. A host carried out of the Hollow alive erupts about 3 days later, and if the Speaker dies first, the Voice is Orphaned (both described in the lore file). The Speaker can choose a host as the target of a creature's forced attack from its Aura of Violence.

#### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The Speaker makes two Whirling Blades attacks.

***Whirling Blades.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 2) Slashing damage plus 5 (2d4) Necrotic damage.

***Howling Wind (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 13 (3d8) Thunder damage, and the target has the Frightened condition for 1 minute. While Frightened this way, the target has the Deafened condition, can't cast spells with Verbal components, and is forced to shriek along with the Speaker. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. *Success:* Half damage only.

***Lure.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 14, one creature within 30 feet that can see the Speaker while it is in a guise. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition for 1 minute. While Charmed this way, the target must spend its movement approaching the guise, can't willingly attack it, and Whirling Blades attack rolls against the target have Advantage. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, and the effect ends early if the target takes damage from a source other than the Speaker, or if an ally within 5 feet uses the Utilize action to shake the target free. *Success:* The target is immune to Lure for 24 hours.

#### Bonus Actions

***Borrowed Face.*** The Speaker chooses one creature it can see within 60 feet, reads a Humanoid that creature loved from its memory, and magically assumes that Humanoid's shape, or returns to its true form. While in the guise:
- The Speaker is Medium, has a Speed of 30 feet, and has no Fly Speed.
- Its Aura of Violence is inactive, and it can't use Whirling Blades, Multiattack, or Howling Wind.
- It doesn't speak except for one quiet, tempting sentence, said to the chosen creature. It has no other voice.
- A creature within 30 feet that succeeds on a DC 16 Wisdom (Insight or Perception) check notices the borrowed face's shadow falls at the wrong angle. That creature has Advantage on saving throws against Lure.

The guise ends if the Speaker takes damage, uses an action other than Lure, or the chosen creature dies. It ends by itself when the Speaker chooses (no action required).

**Habitat:** Planar (Shadowfell), Urban (Kırıkbağ, Ravonia) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Treasure:** None

*Turn Undead:* Speaker'ın **Frightened bağışıklığı** var. **⬜ DM kararı (öneri):** Turn Undead onun üzerinde **çalışmaz.** Not: 2024 Turn Undead hem Frightened hem Incapacitated verir; katı okumada yalnızca Frightened kısmı bağışıktır ⬜ *(kural metnini masada doğrula)*. ## CR Doğrulaması

```
Hedef: CR 5   (benchmark: AC 15 · HP 95 · attack +7 · DC 15 · DPR 35 · PB +3)

D-CR
  Taban HP 67 (9d12 + 9) — benchmark HP'sinin %70'i (resmî CR 6 için 75'ti)
  × 1.25  3+ hasar tipine Resistance (B/P/S + Thunder)
  × 1.15  1–2 hasar tipine Immunity (Necrotic, Poison)
          (cr-math §3: en büyük iki çarpan uygulanır; Legendary Resistance 1/Day ≈ ×1.05 ve
           condition bağışıklıkları yutuldu — "üst üste bindirme")
          67 × 1.25 × 1.15 = 96
  = 96    → tablo CR 5 (95)
  AC 14 vs CR 5 beklentisi 15 → fark 1, düzeltme yok
  D-CR = 5

O-CR   (2 hedefe vuran alan etkileri ×2; Aura'da 15 ft içinde 2 hedef varsayıldı)
  Tur 1: Howling Wind 13 (3d8) × 2 hedef (AoE ×2)                 = 26
         + Aura of Violence 7 (2d6) × 2 hedef                     = 14      → 40
  Tur 2: Multiattack 2 × (9 + 5)                                  = 28
         + Aura                                                   = 14      → 42
  Tur 3: Howling Wind yeniden şarj (~2 kullanım / 3 tur) 26 + Aura 14      → 40
  3 tur ortalaması = (40 + 42 + 40) / 3 = 40.7                    ≈ 41
  → tablo CR 6 (41)
  Attack +5 vs CR 6 beklentisi +7 → −2 → −1 CR
  Save DC 14 vs beklenen 15 → −1, düzeltme yok
  (cr-math §2: attack VEYA save DC, çift sayılmaz)
  O-CR = 6 − 1 = 5

CR = (5 + 5) / 2 = 5   ✅ hedefe oturdu   (5×L3 High = 2,000 XP; CR 5 = 1,800)
```

**Hesaba katılmayanlar (bilerek, DM bilsin):**
- **Lure** hasar vermez ve bir *aksiyon* yer (DPR-nötr). Charm'lı hedefe Blades'in Advantage'ı
  ≈ +2 effective attack; ama Lure yalnız Medium guise'dayken kullanılır, guise **hasarla bozulur**
  ve Borrowed Face'i yeniden almak bonus action ister → net etki küçük, bu yüzden sayılmadı.
- **Aura'nın zorladığı saldırı** (dost ateşi) ~ 7–10 hasar eder; 7 (2d6) Psychic ile aynı büyüklükte
  varsayıldı. Partinin Reaction'ı da harcanır (Shield, Opportunity Attack) — gerçek etkisi
  hesaptan biraz **yüksek**; ⬜ oyun testinde bak.
- Resmî statblock'a göre nerf: **HP 75 → 67, DC 15 → 14, Blades +6 → +5, 3d6+3 + 3d4 → 2d6+2 + 2d4,
  Howling 4d8 → 3d8, Legendary Resistance 1/Day eklendi** (Legendary Resistance, Frightened/Charmed'a
  bağışık bir bossun *Stunned/Incapacitated* ile tek turda kilitlenmesini önlemek için). AC 13 → **14**:
  bir eksende (HP, hasar) indirdik, AC'yi +1 yükselttik (cr-math §6.1) — böylece D-CR 5'te kalır.

**Denge notu:** HP benchmark'ın %70'inde; B/P/S direnci L3 partide (sihirli silahsız) hasarı yarıya
indirir, yani *gerçek* dayanıklılık HP'nin ~1.5 katı — ama 5 kişilik parti ve caster'lar bunu tolere eder
(3–4 tur). Asıl tehdit **Aura'nın aksiyon ekonomisini bozması** ve **Howling Wind'in verbal
caster'ları susturması**; ölümcül hasar değil.
